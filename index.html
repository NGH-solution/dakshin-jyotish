FAQ
Do I need to create an account?

No. Dakshin Jyotish has no email/password sign-up — you get an anonymous recovery code you can use to restore your data on another device.

Does the AI chat need internet?

Yes. AI astrologer chat, daily rashifal and tarot readings are generated by a server and require an active internet connection.

Are the readings real astrological advice?

No — Dakshin Jyotish is for entertainment and self-reflection only. It's not a substitute for professional medical, legal or financial advice.

What languages does it support?

English, Hinglish, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu and Kannada — switch anytime from Settings.

Is Dakshin Jyotish free?

Yes, free to download and use, supported by ads.
